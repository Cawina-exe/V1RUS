# V1RUS

**V1RUS** is a strategy game where the player takes the role of a virus attempting to spread and eliminate humanity while resisting waves of healers and defenses. The player must survive while completing minigames that interfere with the virus’ ability to spread. 

The game mixes **strategy mechanics, wave survival, and arcade-style minigames**, creating a dynamic experience where the player must balance infection, defense, and puzzle challenges.
Developed for **GitHub GameOff Jam 2025**.



# Game Overview

In **V1RUS**, players control a powerful virus trying to spread across the world while humanity fights back.

The virus must survive waves of healers and defensive systems that attempt to stop the infection.

During gameplay, players must also complete **minigames that interfere with the virus spreading**, forcing them to multitask between strategic decisions and arcade-style challenges. 

To succeed, the player must:

- Spread the virus across the environment  
- Survive waves of enemies and defenses  
- Complete minigames that challenge the infection process  
- Adapt strategy as human resistance increases  



#  Key Features

## Virus Strategy Gameplay
Control the spread of the virus while defending against countermeasures.

## Wave Defense System
Fight against waves of healers and defensive units trying to eliminate the virus.

## Arcade Minigames
Minigames interrupt the spread of the virus, forcing the player to react quickly.

## Puzzle Elements
Players must solve gameplay challenges that affect how the virus expands.

## Hybrid Visual Style
Combination of **2D and 3D elements** with retro-inspired arcade gameplay. 


# Gameplay Mechanics

- Strategy-based virus spreading
- Enemy wave survival
- Minigame interruptions
- Increasing difficulty
- Player decision making under pressure
